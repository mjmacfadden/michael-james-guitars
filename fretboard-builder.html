Fretboard Builder

Design a 3D-printable fretboard for your cigar box guitar, with the frets and nut printed right in. It attaches with #6 flat-head screws through countersunk holes at the fret-marker positions, so when the strings wear the frets down you unscrew it, print another and screw it back on.

Printing notes

  • Print flat-side down, no supports. 0.2 mm layers are fine; 0.12 mm gives smoother fret tops and is the practical choice for a radiused board.
  • Use 3+ walls and 40%+ infill so the screw holes stay strong. PLA works; PETG lasts longer under string wear.
  • Two-piece boards: butt the seam faces together on the neck and let the screws (and the alignment pins) register the joint.
  • Drill pilot holes in the neck (1/16″ in hardwood, 3/32″ in softwood) and use #6 × 3/4″ flat-head wood screws, snug but not gorilla-tight.
  • The nut slots are starter slots: a few strokes with a nut file or folded sandpaper dials in the action.
